People

Rider and pedestrians are Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (Male_Adult_01, Male_Adult_03, Male_Adult_08, Male_Adult_14, Female_Adult_02, Female_Adult_05, Female_Adult_11, Female_Adult_14) with the walk, look-around, talk, phone and sit-on-chair animations, © Microsoft Corporation, MIT License (see assets/models/people/LICENSE-Rocketbox.txt). Converted to glTF in Blender for this game: textures reduced to 1K, walk cycles retargeted in place, and a seated pose baked for the rider.

Trees

Generated with ez-tree by Dan Greenheck, MIT License (vendor/ez-tree/LICENSE), including its bark and leaf textures.

Distant city (matte painting)

"Les toits de Paris, panorama - août 2015" by Fred PO, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0. Adapted: cropped to the horizon band, sky removed, hazed and wrapped around the horizon (assets/matte/paris_rooftops.png is shared under the same licence).

Cars

Modelled in Blender for this game (tools/paris_cars.py) at real supermini, saloon and small-van sizes.

Power chair

Modelled in Blender for this game from a script, at the published overall dimensions of a Permobil F5 Corpus (width, length, wheel size, seat height); not a copy of the manufacturer's design.

Skate plaza and streets

Obstacles, kerbs, gutters and road camber are generated in code at real dimensions.

Sky

three.js Sky add-on (MIT), physically based atmosphere.

Rendering

Three.js r169 and its add-ons (EffectComposer, GTAOPass, UnrealBloomPass, SMAAPass, BufferGeometryUtils, GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils), MIT. three-gpu-pathtracer 0.0.23 and three-mesh-bvh, MIT. Photo mode uses progressive WebGL2 path tracing; gameplay uses rasterization with HDR lighting, ground-truth ambient occlusion, bloom, anti-aliasing and colour grading.

Architecture, trees, most street furniture, the tower and window interiors are generated in code. Street lamps, café furniture, planters, the rider and pedestrians are scanned or authored 3D models, and cobble, bark, slate, granite, gravel, stone, concrete, asphalt and fabric surfaces are scans. The far city is a photographic matte painting (see above). This is not an AAA production.
