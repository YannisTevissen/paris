MAKE YOUR OWN LINE

Built to go beyond.

Four controls. About 50 km/h on the motor, 90+ on nitro. Your momentum does the rest.

WASD / Arrows
Drive & steer. S brakes, then reverses. Release to coast.
Space
Jump / hop off a bar
Shift
Nitro boost
Hold E + direction
Lean front, rear, left or right
F / G in the air
Front flip / back flip. Press again for another turn; land it level.
Joystick
Press anywhere on the view and drag, like a power-chair stick: forward drives, back reverses, sideways turns (on the spot when stopped). Let go to stop. Right-drag leans, right click or wheel click jumps, scroll up for nitro. Gamepads work too: left stick, A jump, RT nitro, LB lean.

Build some speed, then hold E and a direction to lift the wheels. Tap to balance; release early to settle back down. Lean too far and you’ll tip over.

Bars catch automatically when you descend onto them in line. Keep your balance and carry your momentum to the end.

Hard impacts and awkward landings can end a run. Reset with R; change view with C. Use the photo button to pause and trace light. Asset credits.
